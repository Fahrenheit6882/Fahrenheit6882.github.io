What's Inside

Six parts, zero magic (okay, maybe a little).

Arduino Nano

The brain. Reads the sensor, cues the sound, and drives the light show.

PIR Motion Sensor

The lookout. Senses body heat moving in from about a foot away.

JQ6500 Sound Module

The voice. Plays a pre-loaded spooky sound effect through the speaker.

WS2812B RGB LEDs

The glow. Two or three LEDs flash and pulse up through the pumpkin lid.

3W Speaker

The howl. Faces downward so sound bounces around inside the pumpkin.

4×AA Battery Cradle

The fuel. A 3D-printed cradle with spring contacts, good for 15–20+ hours.

Who Built This

Fahrenheit Robotics Team 6882

We're a FIRST Robotics team based in Fredericksburg, Virginia, formed in 2017. We welcome public, private, and homeschool high school students (and ambitious 8th graders) from Fredericksburg and the surrounding area. We meet all year, learning not just how to build a competition robot, but how to manage and run a successful team.

The Pumpkinator is one of our student-led fundraisers. Every kit given out helps cover travel, parts, and competition fees for the season ahead.
